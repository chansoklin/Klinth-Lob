My Story

Hello, I'm Klinth — born and raised in Cambodia. Growing up, my mother's kitchen was the heart of our home. The aroma of lemongrass, coconut milk, and kroeung paste filled the air every morning. Those scents weren't just cooking — they were love, memory, and identity.

I created Klinth Eats to preserve those flavors before they faded. But my curiosity didn't stop at Cambodia's borders. I traveled through Japanese ramen shops, Korean barbecue houses, Vietnamese pho kitchens, and Italian trattorias. Everywhere I went, I learned that food is the universal language — one that welcomes everyone to the table.

This website is my digital cookbook. It's also my love letter to my mother, to Cambodia, and to every cook who has ever shared a meal with a stranger. My mission is simple: preserve authentic Khmer recipes, explore global cuisine respectfully, and bring people together — one recipe at a time.

— Klinth

About Klinth Eats

Hello! I'm Klinth — a Cambodian food lover on a mission to share my culture's beautiful cuisine and explore flavors from around the world.

Klinth Eats was born from a simple idea: food brings people together. Whether it's my grandmother's secret Fish Amok recipe or a new dish I discovered while traveling, I want to preserve and share these flavors with you.

Here you'll find:

  • Authentic Khmer recipes from Cambodia
  • Asian cuisine from Japan, Korea, and more
  • European classics I've learned to love
  • Personal stories behind each dish

Thank you for visiting. Now let's cook something delicious together.

— Klinth
